About PhishAware

Cybersecurity Internship Capstone Project • Task 5 Phishing Awareness Simulation & Incident Response

Project Purpose

PhishAware was developed as a comprehensive cybersecurity internship capstone project to demonstrate the complete lifecycle of a controlled phishing-awareness exercise and security operations center (SOC) incident response.

The platform bridges the gap between theoretical social engineering concepts and hands-on operational defense, allowing organizations and cybersecurity students to study attack indicators, measure employee vulnerability, and execute structured incident containment.

Safety & Ethics Guarantees

Strict Educational Constraints:
  • Zero Credential Harvesting: No passwords or authentication credentials are ever prompted, accepted, or stored.
  • Synthetic Test Users: All simulation mailboxes and interaction profiles are local synthetic fixtures.
  • No External Email Dispatches: No unsolicited emails are transmitted across the public internet.
  • Private Lab Environment: Runs entirely on localhost/private network.

Core Competencies Demonstrated

1. Social Engineering Awareness

Realistic phishing scenario creation highlighting urgency, spoofed headers, and domain masking.

2. Telemetry & Monitoring

Event ingestion logging sent, opened, clicked, and reported interactions into SQLite.

3. Incident Response Lifecycle

Execution of Detection, Analysis, Containment, Eradication, and Recovery workflows.

4. Secure Full-Stack Engineering

Python Flask, SQLAlchemy ORM, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js, and parameterized queries.

5. Technical Documentation

Comprehensive project plans, methodology, incident logs, and presentation outlines.

6. Professional Reporting

Executive summaries, forensic timelines, and PDF-ready audit reports.

Technology Stack

Backend: Python 3.13, Flask 3.1
Database: SQLite 3, SQLAlchemy
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5
Visualization: Chart.js 4.4
