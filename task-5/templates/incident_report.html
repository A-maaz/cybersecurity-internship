5. Lessons Learned

  • Rapid Employee Reporting is Crucial: Users who reported the email enabled incident responders to initiate containment within minutes of campaign deployment.
  • Need for Display Name & Domain Verification: Clicking users failed to inspect the sender domain, focusing solely on the "IT Security" display label.
  • Artificial Urgency Remains Highly Effective: Coercive deadlines induced anxiety, leading individuals to bypass standard verification protocols.

6. Strategic Recommendations & Mitigations

Mandatory MFA: Enforce FIDO2 / hardware token authentication to neutralize credential theft attempts.
Email Authentication: Implement rigorous SPF, DKIM, and DMARC (p=reject) controls to stop spoofing.
One-Click Reporting: Provide an integrated webmail button for immediate SOC escalation.
Continuous Simulation: Conduct monthly randomized phishing drills to build behavioral immunity.
